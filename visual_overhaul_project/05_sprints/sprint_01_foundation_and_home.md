## Exit Criteria

- `visual_foundation_decisions.md` is approved enough for implementation.
- Shared style entry points exist if implementation began.
- Home screen has improved layout, action hierarchy, cards, and empty state.
- Light/dark smoke check is documented.
- Any unhandled home issues are split into follow-up stories.

## Review Questions

- Does the foundation solve actual audit problems?
- Did home screen work preserve all actions and group/archive behavior?
- Are shared style entry points small and practical?
- What should be reused by test-taking and results in Sprint 2?

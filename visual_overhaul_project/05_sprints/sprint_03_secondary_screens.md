# Sprint 3: Secondary Screens

## Sprint Goal

Bring editor, history, analytics, review, and high-value dialogs into the same
visual language without sacrificing dense productivity workflows.

## Candidate Stories

- `04_stories/STORY-013_editor_layout.md`.
- Split stories created from `04_stories/STORY-014_history_and_analytics_polish.md`
  after `R-007` is complete.
- Split stories created from `04_stories/STORY-015_review_and_dialog_polish.md`
  after `R-007` and `R-008` are complete.

junior-ready assignments or are explicitly assigned to a senior owner.

## Exit Criteria

- Editor organization and form hierarchy are improved.
- History rows and analytics charts/cards match the foundation.
- Review flow and selected dialogs are visually consistent.
- Empty/loading/no-data states are intentional.
- Any native dialogs left unchanged are documented as MVP choices or post-MVP
  follow-ups.

## Review Questions

- Did dense screens remain efficient?
- Are chart colors readable in both modes?
- Are dialogs clear without creating a new dialog framework?
- Are any secondary screens still visibly default CustomTkinter output?

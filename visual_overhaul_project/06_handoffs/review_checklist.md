# Review Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing a completed visual story.

## Scope

- The story or research task passed the relevant Definition of Ready gate before
  assignment.
- The work matches the assigned story.
- No unrelated screens were changed.
- No service, database, scoring, import/export, or session behavior changed
  unless explicitly approved.

## Visual Quality

- Primary action is clear.
- Secondary and utility actions are lower emphasis.
- Destructive actions are recognizable but not overemphasized.
- Text hierarchy is readable.
- Spacing, alignment, radius, and borders match the foundation.
- Empty/loading/error states in scope are intentional.
- Light and dark mode are readable.
- Minimum window size remains usable.

## Behavior

- Existing callbacks still fire.
- Navigation arguments are unchanged.
- State-specific behavior still works.
- Relevant tests passed or skipped with a reason.

## Documentation

- Story status and handoff are updated.
- Context index and status board reflect any readiness changes.
- Context summaries touched by the story are updated.
- New risks or follow-ups are documented.
